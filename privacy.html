StudyBuddy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026  ·  Applies to the StudyBuddy web app and Android app
In one line: StudyBuddy stores your study data only on your own device. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, and no servers of ours. We do not collect, see, or sell your data.

1. What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. StudyBuddy has no backend server and no tracking. Your tasks, notes, flashcards, syllabus, timer sessions, calendar and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. If you delete the app or clear your browser data, that information is removed from your device.

2. Your API key and the AI doubts bot

The doubts bot and the "make notes / make flashcards" features can use an AI provider. This is optional and off until you add a key.

  • If you enter an API key, it is stored locally on your device only and is sent directly to the provider you chose.
  • When you ask a question (or generate notes/flashcards), the text of that question — and, if you have enabled "use my notes as context", the relevant note text — is sent directly from your device to that provider (Sarvam AI or OpenAI) to produce the answer.
  • We are not involved in that transfer and never receive a copy. The provider's own privacy policy governs that data.
  • If you do not add a key, the bot works fully offline by searching your saved notes.

3. Files you upload

When you upload a chapter (PDF, Word, text, markdown or a photo), the file is read inside your browser on your device to extract its text. The file is not uploaded to us. A photo is processed by an in-browser text-recognition (OCR) library; the reader libraries for PDF/Word/OCR are downloaded from a public content-delivery network the first time you use them.

4. Permissions

  • Storage — to save your data locally on the device.
  • Internet — only to load the app, to fetch the reader libraries, and (if you enable it) to reach the AI provider you chose.

StudyBuddy does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any advertising identifier.

5. Children's privacy

StudyBuddy is designed for school students (roughly classes 6–12) and is safe for them: it collects no personal information, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features and no chat with strangers. The optional AI bot requires a key that an adult would normally set up, and only the text the student types is sent to the chosen provider.

6. Analytics, ads and cookies

None. StudyBuddy uses no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking cookies.

7. Your control over your data

You can export a full backup of your data or erase everything at any time from Settings → Data ("Export backup", "Reset all").

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
